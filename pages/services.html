Our Medical Services

Every service at St. Luke Hospital is delivered by qualified professionals in a clean, private, and welcoming environment.

Gynaecology & Obstetrics (GYN/OBGYN)

St. Luke Hospital's GYN/OBGYN unit is led by Dr. Patience T. Asor, a specialist in women's health with extensive clinical experience. We provide comprehensive gynaecological care in a private, respectful, and professional environment.

Whether you are seeking routine screening, managing a specific condition, or need specialist obstetric care, our team is equipped to support you at every stage.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Gynaecological examinations
  • •Cervical cancer screening (Pap smear)
  • •Menstrual disorder management
  • •Fibroid assessment and management
  • •Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
  • •Infertility evaluation
  • •Menopausal care
  • •STI screening and management

Antenatal & Maternity Care

Our maternity unit provides safe, supportive, and personalised care for expectant mothers from the earliest stages of pregnancy through to postnatal recovery.

We guide mothers and their families through every step of the pregnancy journey with expertise, empathy, and the highest standard of clinical care.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Antenatal booking and registration
  • •Regular antenatal check-ups
  • •Ultrasound scanning
  • •High-risk pregnancy management
  • •Labour monitoring and support
  • •Safe delivery (vaginal and caesarean)
  • •Postnatal care and review
  • •Newborn care and assessment

General Medical Consultation

Our general consultation service provides thorough medical assessments, accurate diagnoses, and effective treatment plans for patients of all ages and with a wide range of conditions.

Our physicians take the time to listen carefully to your concerns, explain findings clearly, and work with you to develop the best plan for your health.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •General health assessments
  • •Chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension)
  • •Infectious disease treatment
  • •Respiratory conditions
  • •Gastrointestinal complaints
  • •Skin conditions
  • •Referrals to specialist services
  • •Preventive care and health advice

Counselling Services

At St. Luke Hospital, we recognise that good health encompasses mental and emotional wellbeing, not just physical health. Our counselling services provide a safe, confidential, and non-judgmental space for patients to address personal and emotional challenges.

All sessions are conducted with absolute privacy and respect.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Grief and bereavement counselling
  • •Relationship and family counselling
  • •Stress and anxiety management
  • •Post-natal depression support
  • •Trauma and crisis counselling
  • •Chronic illness emotional support
  • •Substance use counselling
  • •Adolescent and youth counselling

Surgical Services

St. Luke Hospital's surgical unit is equipped to perform a range of elective and emergency surgical procedures. All procedures are carried out by experienced surgeons in a fully equipped theatre, with complete anaesthetic and post-operative care.

Patient safety and comfort are our highest priorities throughout the surgical journey.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Minor surgical procedures
  • •Abdominal surgery
  • •Gynaecological surgery
  • •Caesarean sections
  • •Hernia repair
  • •Appendicectomy
  • •Wound care and debridement
  • •Post-operative recovery care

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Our in-house laboratory provides fast, accurate, and reliable diagnostic testing to support clinical decision-making. Results are processed promptly and handled with complete confidentiality.

We offer a comprehensive range of tests for routine health checks and specific diagnostic needs.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Full blood count (FBC)
  • •Blood chemistry and metabolic panels
  • •Liver and kidney function tests
  • •Thyroid function tests
  • •Pregnancy tests
  • •Sexually transmitted infection (STI) panels
  • •Malaria and typhoid testing
  • •Urinalysis and microscopy

Pharmacy Services

Our fully stocked on-site pharmacy ensures that patients can access their prescribed medications quickly and conveniently after consultations.

Our qualified pharmacists are available to provide counselling on medications, explain correct dosage and administration, and advise on potential interactions.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Dispensing of prescribed medications
  • •Over-the-counter medications
  • •Patient medication counselling
  • •Dosage and administration advice
  • •Drug interaction checks
  • •Chronic disease medication management
  • •Maternal and antenatal supplements
  • •Paediatric medication dispensing

Emergency Care

Our emergency unit operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It is staffed by trained clinicians equipped to assess and manage acute medical emergencies with speed, precision, and calm professionalism.

In a medical emergency, call us immediately on 080 9444 1515. Do not delay.

Conditions & Services Covered

  • •Acute medical emergencies
  • •Trauma and injury management
  • •Obstetric emergencies
  • •Paediatric emergencies
  • •Respiratory distress
  • •Cardiac events (stabilisation)
  • •Severe infection and sepsis
  • •Post-surgical emergencies
